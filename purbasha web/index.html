An AI and sensor-driven decision support prototype designed to optimize sugarcane water consumption, prevent water stress, and support precision agriculture in water-intensive cropping regions.

The Core Problem in Sugarcane Cultivation

Sugarcane is one of the highest water-consuming crops, requiring 1,500 to 2,500 mm of water throughout its cycle. In practice, irrigation decisions are commonly made using farmer intuition or rigid time intervals. This often results in over-irrigation (leaching soil nutrients, electricity wastage) or under-irrigation (stunting tillering and Grand Growth cane elongation), leading to substantial yield losses.

Simple 3-Step Advisory Workflow

How field telemetry transforms into actionable farmer guidance

1

Field Data

Continuous soil moisture sensors (15cm/30cm depths), local weather station feeds, soil texture mapping, and crop stage tracking.

2

AI Analysis

Dynamic evapotranspiration (ETc) calculation, moisture depletion threshold modeling, and 7-day rainfall forecast integration.

3

Irrigation Advisory

Specific, actionable instructions for each plot: exact start time, runtime duration, pump assignment, and human-in-the-loop control.

Built Strictly for Case Study Requirements

Practical agricultural engineering principles, zero fluff

Plot-Specific Tailoring

Irrigation is tailored to individual plot conditions. Plot A (Grand Growth, Sandy Loam) receives a different recommendation than Plot C (Tillering, Medium Black).

Weather & Rainfall Adaptation

Avoids pumping when rain is imminent. Forecasted precipitation dynamically reduces or delays scheduled irrigation intervals.

Human-in-the-Loop Control

The farmer retains ultimate authority. Ramesh can accept, modify pump durations, or override recommendations with real-time feedback logging.
