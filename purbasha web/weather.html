How Weather Directs Irrigation Schedules (Case Study KJS-AGR-01)

In traditional farming, pumps are turned on based on habit even when rainfall is imminent, causing water waste, energy expense, and waterlogging. SUGARCANE360 prevents this by factoring incoming precipitation:

☀️ Low Rain Probability (<30%)
Scheduled irrigation proceeds normally based on soil moisture depletion.
🌧️ Significant Rainfall (>50% or ≥10mm)
Pumping duration is automatically reduced or postponed (e.g. Saturday 14mm rain event triggers irrigation hold).
