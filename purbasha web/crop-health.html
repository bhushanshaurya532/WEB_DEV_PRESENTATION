🌾 Grand Growth Stage Priority (Days 120–270)

70% to 80% of total sugarcane water is consumed during this critical stage. Internodes lengthen quickly, leaf area reaches maximum canopy spread, and transpiration peaks. Any water deficit during this window causes irreversible cane shortening and permanent tonnage reduction.

Agronomic Impact on Advisory Model:
The AI model scales the crop coefficient (\(K_c\)) from 0.45 in early germination up to 1.25 during Grand Growth. When combined with Kolhapur's 29°C temperature, this triggers frequent, timely 2.5-hour morning watering cycles to maintain root-zone soil moisture between 50% and 70%.
