Sugarcane Crop Water & Irrigation Decision System

SUGARCANE360 is an e-Procurement and sensor-based decision support platform designed exclusively for the sugar sector across Maharashtra and India. It enables sugar factories and sugarcane growers to monitor field moisture, compute crop evapotranspiration (ETc), and schedule precise irrigation timings.

1. Core Problem in Sugarcane Cultivation

Sugarcane requires between 1,500 to 2,500 mm of water per crop cycle. Unplanned irrigation based on intuition leads to over-irrigation (nutrient leaching, pump power loss) or under-irrigation (stunted tiller growth, yield reduction).

2. Simple 3-Step Advisory Workflow for Farmers

Step Module Operation Details
01 Field Sensor Data Continuous monitoring of soil moisture sensors at 15cm and 30cm depths with local weather data.
02 AI Analysis Dynamic Evapotranspiration (ETc) depletion calculations combined with 7-day rainfall forecasts.
03 Farmer Action Clear actionable start time, exact pump duration hours, and manual override control for Ramesh.

3. Key System Features & Technical Capabilities

Plot-Specific Schedule

Calculates distinct watering intervals per plot according to soil texture and growth stage.

Rainfall Integration

Detects incoming precipitation and automatically delays pump runtime to save groundwater.

Human-in-the-Loop

Provides explaining recommendations while letting farmers override or customize hours anytime.
