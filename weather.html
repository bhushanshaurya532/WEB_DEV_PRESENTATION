HOW WEATHER DIRECTS IRRIGATION SCHEDULES (CASE STUDY KJS-AGR-01)

In traditional farming, pumps are turned on based on habit even when rainfall is imminent, causing water waste, energy expense, and waterlogging. SUGARCANE360 prevents this by factoring incoming precipitation into its decision model:

Low Rain Probability (<30%)
Scheduled irrigation proceeds normally based on soil moisture depletion.
Significant Rainfall (>50% or ≥10mm)
Pumping duration is automatically reduced or postponed to preserve groundwater.
